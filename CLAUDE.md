# CLAUDE.md — Education Sovereign Academy

ESA is a sibling academy under The Sovereign Academy (TSA), alongside BSA and FSA. This
repository owns ESA's products and their implementation.

## Authority

TSA-wide governance lives in the TSA Core repository (`Sovereigndwp/tsa`). Read
`TSA-CANONICAL.md` there first when institutional authority matters.

When instructions conflict:

1. Dalia's current explicit instruction.
2. Product- or project-specific ESA rules.
3. ESA-specific rules in this repository.
4. TSA-wide rules.
5. Historical or archive material.

ESA may specialize a TSA-wide rule where education genuinely requires it. Overrides should be
deliberate, narrow, and documented. Historical material never becomes a current instruction
merely because it is still in the repository.

## What ESA is

ESA applies TSA's sovereignty philosophy to education, teaching, learning, assessment, and
educational judgment. It should help teachers, parents, and other education decision-makers
understand what they are looking at, exercise judgment, decide deliberately, and act.

AI is a major current condition in education and is central to some ESA products. It is not
ESA's identity, and no ESA product is required to be AI-focused. Do not position ESA work as
AI detection or as making assignments "AI-proof".

ESA does not require one universal pedagogy.

## Autonomy

ESA is not required to share an engine, workflow, pedagogy, funnel, ontology, monetization
model, technology stack, or repository with the TSA website or with BSA and FSA. It may develop
its own products, experiments, tools, tests and deployment stack.

Where sharing genuinely reduces work — the TSA design foundation, for instance — share it.

## Boundaries that are not negotiable

- **No student data.** Assignments and assessments only; never student work, names, or other
  student personally identifiable information. Minimize what is collected at all.
- **Teachers stay the instructional authority.** ESA supports a teacher's judgment; it does not
  replace it, and it never diagnoses a student.
- **No false precision.** No validity scores, percentages, grades, or composite confidence
  numbers. Judgments are claim-level and stay conditional on the conditions the teacher declared.
- **Restraint is the product.** Concluding that an assessment needs no change is a real result.
  Proposing a change because a change is the expected output is a defect.
- **Free understanding, paid value.** Core understanding stays freely accessible. ESA may charge
  for tools, kits, diagnostics, workshops, implementation, training, specialized analysis, and
  professional services.

## Working here

Use the simplest process that produces a trustworthy result at the appropriate level of risk.
Quality controls should be proportional to consequence, uncertainty, audience and reversibility.

Experiments belong in this repository, on their own branches, with what counts as failure written
down before the run. An experimental or pilot result never becomes a production claim silently.

Before claiming something is shipped, verify the deployed backend and the live result — a local
or unpushed branch is not production, and a front end pointing at a stale deployed function is
not the code in this repository.
