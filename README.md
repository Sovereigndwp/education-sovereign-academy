# Education Sovereign Academy (ESA)

ESA is one of the three sibling academies of **The Sovereign Academy (TSA)**, alongside
Bitcoin Sovereign Academy (BSA) and Financially Sovereign Academy (FSA).

ESA applies TSA's sovereignty philosophy to **education**: teaching, learning, assessment,
and the judgment teachers and parents exercise about them — including in an era where AI can
perform parts of the work a task was meant to evidence.

## The question ESA works on

> What does this assessment actually let me conclude about what my students know — and do I
> need to change anything at all?

"This is already doing its job, I would leave it alone" is a successful answer, not a failure
to find something.

## What lives here

This repository is ESA's own implementation home: its products, experiments, tools, research,
harnesses and deployment material. ESA is autonomous in how it builds — it is not required to
share an engine, a workflow, a pedagogy, a funnel, a technology stack or a repository with the
TSA website or with the other academies.

## Governance

ESA inherits the TSA-wide principles that apply to it — truthfulness, dignity, privacy and data
minimization, accessibility, professional boundaries, human authority over consequential
decisions, and the shared design foundation, where ESA's accent is blue.

TSA Core governance lives in the private `Sovereigndwp/tsa` repository. Read `TSA-CANONICAL.md`
there first when institutional authority matters. See `CLAUDE.md` for how to work in this
repository.

## Status

Early. The current work is a small invited teacher pilot with pre-registered falsification
conditions. Nothing here should be described as validated until that pilot says so.
