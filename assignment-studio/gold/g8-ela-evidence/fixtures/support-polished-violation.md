# Author's Point of View — Evidence Paragraph (Support version)

**Grade 8 English Language Arts · Informational Text · One class period**

### Before you read

Key words: **span** = the part of a bridge that stretches across the water · **inspection** = an official check of a structure's condition · **commuters** = people who travel to work every day · **deplored** = strongly disapproved of.

### Read the passage

*(The passage "The Bridge on Mill Street" is unchanged — read it in full on the attached page.)*

### Step 1 — Find the author's point of view

The author believes the town should think carefully before replacing the bridge, because the bridge matters to the town in ways the engineer's report leaves out. In your own words, write one sentence stating this point of view:

> The author believes ______________________________________________.

### Step 2 — Evidence

Choose **two** of the sentences below that show the author's point of view. Copy them into the boxes.

- "The steel is tired."
- "the only place in town where you can stand over moving water"
- "A new bridge would last seventy-five."
- "inspection reports measure steel. They do not measure what a town does on a bridge"
- "Every graduating class since 1974 has taken its photograph on it."

### Step 3 — Explain how

For each piece of evidence, finish the sentence:

> The author includes this because it shows ______________________________.

### Step 4 — The other side

The engineer's case for replacement is that the bridge is old and narrow, and a new bridge would last longer. Finish this sentence:

> The author responds to the engineer's case by ______________________________.

### Step 5 — Put it together

Copy your five sentences from Steps 1–4 into one paragraph. Add a closing sentence.

**Scoring (4-point rubric):** Point of view clearly stated and accurate (1) · Two relevant pieces of evidence (1) · Explanation connects each piece of evidence to the point of view (1) · Accurately describes how the author handles the opposing view (1).
