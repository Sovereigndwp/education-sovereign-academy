Assignment Studio adapts an assignment for the students in front of you while protecting what you intended them to learn — and shows you exactly what changed, what it protected, and what to check.

First we read your intent. Then we change only what you allow.

Before anything is adapted, we show you the assignment's Learning Contract — what students must understand, what they must actually think through, what has to appear in their work for you to know it happened, and the constraints you're working under. You confirm it or correct it. That confirmation governs every version we make.

Three versions are available today:

  • Support — improve access without making the intellectual task easier. Directions, chunking, vocabulary, layout, scaffolds. Never the reasoning being assessed.
  • Advanced — deepen the same learning rather than adding more work. Abstraction, transfer, justification, interacting variables. Never “five more questions”.
  • Make Thinking More Visible — make student reasoning observable without turning every task into an oral defense or a grading marathon. We don't claim anything is AI-proof.

Every version comes with its receipt.

Side by side with your original, each version shows what we changed and why, what we protected, and a short “check this” list — anything we're not certain we preserved, or any change that could plausibly alter what you're measuring. When we aren't sure, we say review required rather than pretend.

Then we ask one question: did this version change something students need to know, think through, or demonstrate that you wanted preserved? Your answer is how the studio learns your judgment, not the other way round.

How it works

  1. Upload one assignment. Word, PDF, a photo, or pasted text. Optionally tell us in one sentence what students should learn from it.
  2. Confirm or correct the Learning Contract. Four short fields. Nothing changes until you confirm.
  3. Choose Support, Advanced, or Make Thinking More Visible. Add classroom constraints in your own words if you like — “I can't grade anything extra.”
  4. Compare, edit, download. Original beside the new version, the preservation trace beneath. Edit in place. Download Word or print to PDF. Make another version from the same assignment.

What we never do

  • No student names, work, or grades. We adapt the assignment, never anything about a student. There is no field for a diagnosis or a label, and no rule that turns one into a change.
  • Your assignment stays yours. It is not shared with your school or anyone else. Pilot schools receive only aggregate counts teachers are told about beforehand.
  • No training on your material. You can delete an assignment and everything made from it at any time, from its own page.
  • No “AI-proof”. No detection, no surveillance. Visible thinking is about better evidence, not policing.
