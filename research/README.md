# ESA research and product knowledge

Migrated from TSA Core (`Sovereigndwp/tsa`, `projects/teach-back-flywheel/` and
`Claude outputs/`) on 2026-09-14, when ESA became a repository of its own. TSA Core
retains every one of these files in its git history, so provenance is intact on both sides.

- `TRANSFORMATION-CONTRACT-v0.1.md` — the governing product truth for Assignment Studio.
  Superseded contracts are in `_superseded/` and are not to be revived.
- `ESA-SHIP-INVENTORY-2026-09-13.md` — SHIP / HIDE / DELETE inventory; cited by
  `esa/review/PILOT-PRE-REGISTRATION.md` and `assignment-studio/functions/_shared/esa.ts`.
- `ESA-PILOT-INVITE-EMAILS-2026-09-13.md` — five drafted, unsent teacher invitations.
- `ESA-ASSET-INVENTORY-2026-09-11.md` — what education work existed as of 2026-09-11.
- `decisions/` — dated decision and review records from the gold run and the pilot decision.
  RUN-NOTES.md and SCIENCE-FIXTURE-DECISION.md are deliberately absent here: they are
  byte-identical to the copies already preserved under
  `assignment-studio/harness/runs/2026-09-08-20-40-gold-partial/`, and a second canonical
  copy of the same record is a defect.

What deliberately did NOT come here: the Owner Decision Corpus seed
(`ODC-0901..0919`, `SEED-MANIFEST.md`, `OWNER-DECISION-CORPUS-SEED.html`,
`validate-corpus.py`). Those were mined during this project but record TSA-wide rules —
free core, anti-funnel, bitcoin-not-crypto, the youth boundary — and are candidate
precedent for TSA Core's own registry. They stay in TSA Core.

These documents are dated records. Where one describes a rule that has since changed,
it stays as written.
