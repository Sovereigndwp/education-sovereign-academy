The Teach-Back Flywheel

The agentic version of teachers teach teachers. A teacher submits their own lesson or game; an agent pipeline audits it against Dalia's own curriculum standards and prior rulings, upgrades it with a tyb-engine interactive, and publishes it under a revenue-share — so Dalia stops being the sole author and becomes the factory and the seal of quality other teachers build on.

The thesis in one paragraph

Dalia has already built the hard parts as separate machines: a curriculum quality-gate pipeline (build-curriculum), an owner-decision-corpus delegation system (owner-approval-delegation / evidence-based-owner-delegation), a game/interactive engine with formal audit CLIs and telemetry (tyb-engine), a monetization skill set that enforces free-core (sovereign-monetization), and a bilingual EN/ES distribution edge. The Teach-Back Flywheel does not invent a new machine. It wires these machines end-to-end and points the intake at other teachers instead of at Dalia. Every submission that escalates to her becomes a new precedent in the corpus, which raises the auto-approval rate, which lowers her per-unit time toward zero. That compounding is the flywheel.

Design stance. The owner moves up a level, not out of governance (the explicit rule inside owner-approval-delegation). Dalia decides new principles, genuinely new pedagogy, changes of direction, and disputed cases. The system handles application of established principles, routine review, repair, and verification. The flywheel is never presented as removing her from curriculum governance.

Architecture — the end-to-end loop

Seven stages. The arrow from Stage 4 back into the corpus, and from Stage 7 back into the standards, are what make it a flywheel rather than a conveyor belt.

auto (no human) agent repair owner only
1

Teacher intake — the Teacher Desk

A teacher submits their own lesson or HTML game through a Concept Inbox form. Captured: concept + one-line learning objective, target age band and standard, the artifact itself, and acceptance of the contributor license + revenue-share terms. reuse: concept-inbox-form-spec.mdreuse: lesson-inject.js

2

Automated pedagogy audit — the Owner-Proxy run

One orchestrated pass runs the seven build-curriculum gates, the tyb audit CLIs for any interactive, audit-claims for facts, and the Owner-Proxy evaluator against the Owner Decision Corpus, plus the Experience Director for the felt experience. It emits a Gate Report and a single routing verdict.

AUTO-APPROVE AUTO-REPAIR ESCALATE

3

Auto-upgrade with a tyb-engine interactive

The unit is fitted with the tyb interactive layer: telemetry hooks, a misconception map/classifier, and juice/audio/panel polish. If the submitted lesson has no meaningful interaction, one is scaffolded via build-interactive with false-inference risk evaluated. Repairs (missing misconception map, weak interaction, stale data, brand/contrast drift) are applied here — bounded to 2 loops; still-failing units escalate. reuse: tyb skills/ + telemetry + misconceptions.js

4

Owner escalation — edge cases only

Only novel pedagogical decisions, unrepaired conceptual-model misconceptions, boundary/safeguarding violations, low Owner-Proxy confidence, or precedent conflicts reach Dalia. She rules once. Her ruling is written back into the Owner Decision Corpus in the canonical form context → proposed treatment → objection → governing principle → owner decision → accepted treatment → evidence. This is the learning step of the loop.

5

Publish — free-core, under the seal

The student-facing lesson/game is published free on the teacher's desk page, on canonical TSA brand tokens, carrying Verified by The Sovereign Academy and teacher attribution. Free-core is non-negotiable (see monetization). reuse: TSA Token Sheet · bsa-brand-steward

6

Distribute — EN/ES, multi-channel

distribution-run packages the unit for Substack, X, LinkedIn, and nostr with Spanish/LATAM localization; capture-and-route places a capture point on the free interactive → email → nurture toward the paid kit. reuse: distribution-run · capture-and-route

7

Revenue-share accounting + feedback

The paid Teacher Kit sells through Stripe Connect; the split posts automatically to a revenue-share ledger. Meanwhile the published interactive streams wrong-answer telemetry back through the misconception classifier, improving misconception coverage in the standards and feeding the corpus. Corpus grows → auto-approve rate rises → Stage 4 shrinks. reuse: revenue-instrumentation · Stripe · tyb telemetry

The compounding claim, stated plainly. Owner-minutes per published unit is the North-Star cost metric. At cold start it is high (everything escalates). Each escalation adds precedent. When the corpus crosses the maturity threshold for a unit type, AUTO-APPROVE switches on for that type and owner-minutes for it drop toward zero — while quality is held constant by the same gates.

The quality bar — built on the existing gates + corpus, not a new rubric

A submitted unit is scored on the seven build-curriculum gates plus the tyb audit dimension. Each gate returns PASS, REPAIRABLE, or FAIL (P0). The routing verdict is a deterministic function of the gate results and the Owner-Proxy judgment.

AUTO-APPROVE

Publishes with no human. Requires all:

  • every gate PASS (zero P0, zero unrepaired misconception),
  • Owner-Proxy = approve at high confidence,
  • ≥ 3 on-point governing precedents, zero novel decisions,
  • corpus mature for this unit type.
AUTO-REPAIR

Agent fixes, re-runs gates, ≤ 2 loops. Handles the mechanical/known-pattern failures:

  • missing misconception map or weak interaction (tyb upgrade),
  • stale data point, missing teacher-key nuance,
  • brand-token drift, accessibility contrast.

Still failing after 2 loops → escalate.

ESCALATE

Goes to Dalia. Triggered by any of:

  • P0 factual error unresolved,
  • conceptual-model misconception unrepaired,
  • boundary/safeguarding violation,
  • a novel pedagogical choice,
  • Owner-Proxy low confidence or precedent conflict.
Honest cold-start (from owner-approval-delegation). As of 2026-08 the Owner Decision Corpus does not yet meet the maturity threshold (≥ 30 entries, ≥ 20 of them rulings/inferred-from-correction rather than encoded rules, ≥ 3 on-point per unit type). Until then, AUTO-APPROVE is disabled and every unit escalates — and the system says so out loud rather than quietly approving. Week 1 seeds the corpus from existing rulings (the retired membership page = a free-core precedent, the unit-circle pass records, doctrine, and repo corrections). The Owner-Proxy may infer from precedent but may never invent a preference and attribute it to Dalia.

Monetization — free-core, contribution-weighted revenue-share

Free-core rule (hard, encoded as corpus precedent). The student-facing lesson or game is always free. TSA once sold a membership that gated core education; the owner ruled it a violation and retired it. The flywheel monetizes the teacher's supporting kit and desk and institutional licensing — never learner access to the core lesson.

Revenue-share — contribution-weighted

The year-1 dollars are deliberately modest. The asset being built is the library + the seal + the auto-approve rate — marginal cost per additional unit trends toward zero as the corpus matures, so the model is a compounding library, not a services business. How money actually flows

Stripe Connect (Stripe is already in use). Each teacher onboards as a Connected Account. A kit purchase is a single charge that splits automatically via a Connect transfer at the contribution-weighted ratio; TSA is the platform account and keeps its share + reserve. The revenue-share ledger reconciles monthly and is visible to the teacher on their desk. Institutional licenses are invoiced by TSA, with the author-pool royalty distributed from the platform account. Stripe Connect (vs. manual monthly payout) is the recommended mechanism — confirm it fits your current Stripe setup.

Validate before you build (validation ladder). Per offer-design, pre-sell the Teacher Desk to cohort 1 at a founding price and collect LOIs before building full Stripe Connect + the kit generator. If founders won't commit to the desk, the split numbers are moot and cheap to change.
